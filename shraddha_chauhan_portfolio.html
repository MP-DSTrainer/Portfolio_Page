01 — About

Where business questions meet operational data

I'm a Business Analyst with experience across enterprise applications, reporting, data validation, and operational analytics — with a particular depth in retail and supply chain operations: WMS, OMS, inventory, and order-fulfillment workflows.

My work sits at the point where systems, data, and people meet. I run requirements elicitation, process mapping, and gap analysis, document them in BRDs, FRDs, and RTMs, then follow through with SQL data validation, functional and regression testing, and UAT — so what gets built actually matches what the business asked for.

On the analytics side, I build KPI dashboards and automated reporting in Power BI, Tableau, and Excel, backed by SQL and Python — helping teams monitor performance trends and make data-driven decisions.
